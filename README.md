A site made for Obii, killer for Praxis and the undisputed GOAT of competitive Dead by Daylight.

- Obii profile
- 13,109 hours of Dead by Daylight
- Praxis match archive
- Obii Rating
- Advanced analytics
- Comparisons against Xeno, Pedro, Zaka, Wispy, and Angelz
- Propaganda generator with copy-to-Discord button
